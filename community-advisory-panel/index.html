The FAIR and Open Samples leadership team convenes an eight-person Community Advisory Panel comprising leaders from each of the key stakeholder communities involved in this grant. The Panel helps to catalyze community involvement over the duration of the performance period, including socializing project outcomes and aiding in dissemination of project deliverables.

The Panel is kept up-to-date on project progress with quarterly one-hour panel meetings and provides valuable feedback on project execution and deliverables development.

The Open and FAIR Samples project is funded by the U.S. National Science Foundation under the Findable Accessible Interoperable Reusable Open Science (FAIROS) program: "Disciplinary Improvements: Open and FAIR samples: maturing the sample data ecosystem".
